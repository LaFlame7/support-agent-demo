category: account_access

---
Go to https://acme-learn.example/login and choose "Forgot password". We email a reset link that works for 60 minutes. If the email doesn't arrive within 10 minutes, check spam and make sure you use the address you registered with. After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes; the lock clears on its own.
