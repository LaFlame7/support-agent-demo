title: Change or cancel your plan
category: billing
tags: plan, subscription, cancel, upgrade, downgrade, renewal, monthly, annual
---
You can switch between monthly and annual plans or cancel under Settings → Billing → Plan. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the paid period. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Support agents can't change plans on your behalf.
