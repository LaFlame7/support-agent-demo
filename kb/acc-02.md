id: acc-02
title: Sign in with a school or company account (SSO)
category: account_access
tags: sso, school account, company account, single sign-on, google workspace, microsoft
---
If your school or employer gave you access, use "Sign in with your organisation" on the login page instead of a password. Your organisation's admin manages these accounts, so password resets and access removals go through them. If you see "organisation not found", ask your admin for the exact sign-in domain.
